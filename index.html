Recent Projects

These projects are meant to show whats possible with current AI coding tools, and to get in reps to learn about how to best use those tools. This is a practice in learning, not in proving how much code one can write. Each project stems from a real problem I've seen out in the world, from sharing my favorite restaurants to exploring the ecology of a living woodland. Take a look :)

#1

Ben's List

My friends always ask me where to eat. I built a chatbot that gives them recommendations I've curated from over a decade of eating around the world. I still prefer they text me directly, but I wanted to share it with the world.

Started as a basic chat tool. Nuked the UI, went minimal. Went from naively dumping all data into a prompt to a two-stage LLM pipeline resulting in a 6-9x token reduction.

#2

Forage

I wanted to explore a living version of the Marin coast: walk under the oaks, follow a creek to the ocean, and notice how the trees, wildlife, weather and tides fit together.

A first-person ecological survival game built with Claude and Codex. Gather, craft, fish, dive the kelp forest, and see how your choices change the woodland.
